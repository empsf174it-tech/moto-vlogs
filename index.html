Meet Alex Vance

Meet Alex Vance

I started Moto Vlogz in 2018 with a simple goal: to document the parts of the world that highways bypass. For me, motorcycling isn't just about speed; it's about vulnerability, connection, and the raw grit of the trail. I travel solo with a heavy adventure bike, two action cameras, and a drone.

From crossing the desert dunes of South America to negotiating the technical, high-altitude muddy trails of the Himalayas, my channel is a front-row seat to the joys and struggles of extreme touring. No crew, no scripts, just pure adventure.

The Steed

BMW R1250GS Adventure equipped with heavy-duty crash bars, knobby tires, and aluminum luggage.

Miles Logged

Over 180,000 miles ridden across 5 continents, including paved highways and rugged forest trails.

Notable Trip

Completed a solo, unsupported crossing of the Trans-Siberian Highway through remote wilderness.
